01 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences (such as selected timezone or language), and ensure smooth page navigation.

In addition to cookies, Tazkirah Online Education ("Tazkirah", "we", "us") may use web beacons, local storage, and session tokens to enhance online classroom security and user experience.

02 How & Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies strictly to improve educational delivery, secure student evaluation forms, and streamline class scheduling. Specifically, cookies allow us to:

  • Keep parent and student account sessions securely logged in during active class sessions.
  • Remember your country, preferred currency, and local timezone for class scheduling.
  • Prevent fraud, bot submissions, and malicious activity on trial registration forms.
  • Analyze aggregate, non-identifiable platform performance metrics to improve video load times.

03 Categories of Cookies We Deploy

The cookies deployed across our portal ([tazkirahonline.com](https://tazkirahonline.com)) fall into three main categories:

  • 1. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential): Mandatory for fundamental site functionality, navigation, and trial booking form verification. These cannot be switched off in our systems.
  • 2. Preference & Functional Cookies: Enable personalized site features, such as preserving your selected timezone (e.g., GMT, EST, PST, AEST) so class schedules render accurately.
  • 3. Performance & Analytics Cookies: Collect anonymous data on how visitors interact with our pages (e.g., page load speeds, error occurrences). This data contains zero personally identifiable information.

  • Privacy Guarantee: Third-party integrations are strictly governed by their respective privacy policies. Tazkirah never permits third parties to install advertising tracking spyware on our students.

05 Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have the right to accept, refuse, or clear cookies at any time through your web browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact form submissions or classroom functionality:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Site permissions > Cookies and site data.
