02 Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal data necessary to provide high-quality, personalized 1-on-1 online instruction and customer support. The information collected includes:

  • Parent / Adult Account Information: Full name, email address, WhatsApp / phone number, city, country, and preferred timezone.
  • Student Profile Data: Student's first name, age or grade level, current Quranic / Arabic reading proficiency, learning goals, and teacher gender preferences.
  • Schedule & Attendance Records: Selected class times, lesson completion logs, teacher feedback reports, and attendance records.
  • Technical & Session Logs: IP address, device type, browser specifications, and connection logs to verify online classroom stability and security.

03 Dedicated Protection for Children's Data

Because Tazkirah provides Quranic education to children and young learners under 18, protecting minor privacy is our highest priority.

  • Parental Consent Mandatory: No minor under 18 may enrol or provide personal information without explicit parental or legal guardian consent.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We strictly never sell, rent, trade, or monetize any child's personal data or profile information to third parties.
  • Supervised 1-on-1 Classrooms: Live video lessons are conducted in secure, private 1-on-1 online rooms. Parents maintain an open-door policy to join or monitor any session at any time.

04 Lawful Basis & Processing Purposes

We process personal data based on contractual necessity (delivering your enroled courses), legitimate interest (improving teaching quality and safeguarding), and explicit consent (for WhatsApp updates and trial scheduling). Purposes include:

  • Matching students with certified female or male educators based on age, level, and timezone.
  • Sending class reminders, monthly schedule updates, and academic progress reports via WhatsApp and Email.
  • Processing tuition payments securely and providing billing documentation.
  • Maintaining institutional safeguarding and child protection compliance.

05 Third-Party Service Providers

Tazkirah uses trusted third-party technology providers to deliver live online classes and administrative support. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis under binding data processing agreements:

  • Classroom Video Hosting: Secure video conferencing platforms (e.g., Zoom Video Communications, Google Meet) for live 1-on-1 teaching sessions.
  • Communication Channels: WhatsApp Business API and secure email servers for instant support and scheduling notifications.
  • Payment Processors: PCI-DSS compliant financial institutions and gateways. Tazkirah never stores credit or debit card numbers on our local servers.

06 Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including 256-bit SSL encryption, firewalls, and restricted administrative access to safeguard data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Personal data is retained only for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill legal and accounting obligations. Upon account termination or written request, account data is securely deleted or anonymized.

07 Your Rights & How to Exercise Them

Under applicable data protection laws, you possess the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of all personal data held about you or your child.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request temporary restriction of data processing under specific conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email our team at asktazkirah@gmail.com. Access requests are fulfilled free of charge within 30 days.
