Profile & Background

Muhammad Umair completed Hifz-ul-Quran at the age of nine. He went on to advanced study in Tajweed and Qirat — the disciplines of accurate pronunciation and the recognised styles of Quranic recitation — and developed a particular specialisation in Maqamat al-Quran, the traditional melodic modes that give recitation its expressive shape. He holds a Bachelor’s degree from Sargodha University.

He has more than eight years of teaching experience. He taught for two years at Al Saeed Islamia High School and spent three years as a Mu’allim and Qari with an online tuition centre in Islamabad. For the past five years he has taught internationally, working with students in the United States, the United Kingdom, the Gulf region and Italy.
