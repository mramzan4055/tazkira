RIGOROUS SELECTION

How Teachers Earn a Place at Tazkirah

Only 1 in 25 teacher applicants pass our strict 4-phase evaluation process.

1

Senior Recitation Audit

Every applicant recites live in front of senior Qaris to audit Makharij, Tajweed precision, and voice clarity.

2

Direct Credential Check

Qualifications and Sanad chains are directly verified with issuing institutions in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and South Asia.

3

Supervised Teaching Audit

Candidates teach a live supervised session. We evaluate their patience, encouragement, and adaptability with struggling students.

4

Identity & DBS Background Checks

Full government identity verification and two verified references from previous educational institutions.
