SECTION 01

How We Verify Every Educator

Only 1 in 25 teacher applicants pass our strict evaluation protocol before receiving a student of their own.

1. Credential Audit

Qualifications and Sanad chains are directly verified with issuing universities in Egypt (Al-Azhar, Cairo), Saudi Arabia, and South Asia.

2. Identity & Background

Full government identity verification and formal background checks renewed annually for all active teaching faculty.

3. Verified References

Two verified professional references from previous educational institutions confirming teaching ethics and child conduct.

4. Supervised Audit

Candidates teach a live supervised session evaluated for patience, Makharij precision, and encouraging tone with children.

SECTION 02

How Live Sessions Are Conducted

Strict boundary protocols enforced for all online 1-on-1 classes.

🚪

Open Door Parent Access

You are welcome in any class—always. Without asking and without prior notice. Many parents observe the first few weeks and step back once their child has settled comfortably.

📅

Scheduled 1-on-1 Zoom Links

Classes take place strictly at agreed times on monitored academy Zoom links. Teachers never initiate contact outside scheduled lesson hours.

🔒

Centralized Communication Only

Teachers do not exchange personal phone numbers, WhatsApp, or social media handles with students or families. All scheduling runs through Tazkirah administration.

📽️

Recording Privacy Protocol

Classes are never recorded by default. If a session is ever recorded for teacher quality evaluation, we request your written permission first, and files are purged within 30 days.

SECTION 03

Child Data & Information Policy

📥 What We Collect
Your child's first name and age, the course enrolled, lesson schedule, progress notes, and parent contact details.
🛑 What We Do Not Collect
We never ask for your child's school name, home residential address, full date of birth, or national ID documents.
⏳ How Long We Keep It
Information is retained only during active enrolment, plus 12 months after a family leaves, then permanently deleted.
🔐 Who Accesses Data
Strictly limited to your child's assigned teacher and the core administration team handling scheduling. Nobody else.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Safeguarding & Trust FAQs

Are live 1-on-1 classes recorded? +
Not by default. If a session is ever recorded for internal teacher quality audit, we request your explicit written permission first, and any recorded video file is permanently deleted within 30 days.
Can I sit in on my child's class? +
Yes, always. Without asking permission and without advance notice. Parents are welcome to sit in on any session from start to finish.
Are teachers background checked? +
Yes. Every teacher completes government identity verification, two professional references from previous academic institutions, and a live supervised teaching audit, renewed annually.
What student data do you collect? +
We only collect your child's first name, age, course schedule, and teacher progress notes, alongside parent contact details. We never ask for school names, home addresses, dates of birth, or identification documents.
Will you ever post my child's image or voice in marketing? +
Never without explicit written consent. Not on social media, not on our website, and not anywhere else.
