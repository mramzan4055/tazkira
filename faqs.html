Getting Started & Free Trial
How do online 1-on-1 Quran classes work at Tazkirah?
Classes are live, 1-on-1 video sessions with a qualified Sanad teacher, 30 minutes long. The teacher shares the Quranic text on screen, listens to your child read, and gently corrects Tajweed and Makharij in real time.
Is the first class really 100% free with no credit card required?
Yes. Your first 30-minute 1-on-1 session is completely free with the teacher of your choice. Zero credit card details are requested, and there is zero obligation to continue.
What age can my child start learning Quran online?
Most children are ready from around age four or five, once they can focus for a 30-minute stretch. Book a free trial and the teacher will evaluate your child honestly.
What technical setup do we need at home?
All you need is an iPad, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer with a working camera, microphone, and a standard internet connection. No special software download is required.
Teachers, Credentials & Sanad
Can I inspect and choose my child's teacher before paying?
Yes. Every teacher's full name, Cairo / Al-Azhar credentials, languages spoken, and personal teaching philosophy are published transparently on our Teachers page. You select your tutor prior to booking.
Are female teachers available for women and young girls?
Yes. Female teachers holding full Sanad certifications are available across all courses for female students and young children. You can request a female teacher at booking without explanation.
What if my child does not click with their assigned teacher?
Tell us or request a change directly from your dashboard with 1-click. Changing tutors is a completely normal request and costs nothing.
Pricing, Payments & Contracts
How much do monthly plans cost?
We offer four published plans starting from $45/month (2 classes/week) up to $75/month (5 classes/week). There is a one-time $25 setup fee charged only after your free trial if you decide to continue.
Is there a long-term contract or cancellation notice period?
No contracts. No notice periods. You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time without penalty or awkward calls.
What happens if we miss a class due to travel or illness?
Any class rescheduled with 12 hours notice is carried forward. Remaining balances roll over so you never lose what you paid for.
Safety, Safeguarding & Privacy
How are your online classes kept safe for children?
Every teacher completes identity checks and provides two verified references from previous educational institutions before teaching. Parents are welcome to observe any live session. Read our Safeguarding Policy.
Will you ever use my child's photo or audio in marketing?
Never without your explicit written permission. Not on social media, not on our website, not anywhere.
