The registration fee
There is a one-time $25 registration fee. It covers your child's placement assessment and their first learning plan — the work the teacher does between your free class and your first paid one. It is charged once, after your free class, and only if you decide to continue.
Pausing, switching and cancelling
Pausing
Need to pause for a holiday, exams or travel? Any remaining balance carries forward to the following month. Just tell us.
Switching
You can change plan, course or teacher at any point, at no extra cost. Changing teacher does not require an explanation.
Cancelling
No contract. No notice period. Tell us and we will stop.
