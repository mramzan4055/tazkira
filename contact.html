Quick Answers Before You Reach Out

How quickly will I receive a response to my message?
For WhatsApp messages, our UK/US support team usually responds within 15–30 minutes during active hours. For email form submissions, we reply within 2 hours on weekdays.
Can I request a specific teacher when contacting support?
Yes, absolutely. If you prefer a female teacher for yourself or your daughter, or a tutor fluent in a specific language (English, Arabic, Urdu), simply mention it in your message or free trial request.
How do free trial class arrangements work?
Once you request a trial or message us on WhatsApp, we schedule a 30-minute 1-on-1 session at a time that fits your exact schedule. No credit card or registration fee is required.
