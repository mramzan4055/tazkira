The thing most adults are actually worried about

It is not ability. It is being seen struggling.

One-to-one means there is no class, no audience, and nobody else hearing you read. Your teacher has taught adult beginners before and is not surprised by anything you cannot do yet.

Most adults also move faster than they expect through the early stages, because the structure being taught makes sense to an adult mind in a way it does not to a six-year-old.
